# Intro handwriting recording

`intro-inkpen.m4a` is a 4.8-second mono excerpt (3.0–7.8 seconds), level adjusted with short edge fades, from **Writing with inkpen** by **stephan**.

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Writing_with_inkpen.ogg
Original recording: PDSounds #179, April 22, 2007.
License: Public domain, released worldwide by its author. See the source page for the dedication.

Playback volume follows the SVG stroke animation; no synthesized noise is added.
